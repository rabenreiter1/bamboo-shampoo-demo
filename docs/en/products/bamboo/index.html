What is included?

How does the cap work?

The centre is part of the flexible silicone cap. The surface yields when pressed. The ring-mounted closure lifts out of the opening and turns aside.

What do I keep for a refill?

The bamboo shell and silicone cap. Remove the empty PET insert. The new insert rests on the tube rim with its upper flange. Remove its film seal completely before use.

Explore the refill system

THE SYSTEM IN MOTION

ALL IN.

Bamboo shell, silicone cap and a filled insert. The film keeps the new refill sealed.

PEEL IT.

Take the cap off. Lift the film seal and peel it completely off the rim.

PRESS. POUR.

Put the cap back on, open the closure and turn it aside. The flexible silicone centre is part of the cap.

REFILL IT.

Take the cap off. Remove the empty PET insert and put a new refill in.

GO AGAIN.

Keep the bamboo shell and silicone cap. The new refill stays sealed until its next use.
