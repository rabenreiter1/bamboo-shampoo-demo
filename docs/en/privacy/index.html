Privacy

This design preview has no customer accounts, contact forms, payment processing, embedded social networks, analytics or marketing services. Images, fonts and 3D models are served with the website. Your selection and your place in the product journey are stored only in your browser’s sessionStorage and are not sent to us. Stored values are product, scent, quantity, and the chapter and scroll position used to return to your journey. A completed demo checkout also stores a local demo reference, product lines and simulated delivery and payment choices, so the confirmation survives a reload. It stores no personal information or real payment details. GitHub Pages hosts this website and processes technical connection data when you request a page, according to its privacy statement. Operator details are in the legal notice.
