What comes with my first purchase?

For €12.50: the bamboo shell, reusable silicone cap and a filled 250 ml PET insert with a peel-off film seal.

How much is a refill — and what is included?

A 250 ml refill costs €4.50. It contains the filled, transparent PET insert with a film seal. Your bamboo shell and cap are reused.

How do I replace the insert?

Take off the cap, remove the empty insert and put the new refill in. Peel off the film seal completely before use, then put your existing cap back on.
