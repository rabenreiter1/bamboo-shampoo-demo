QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

GOOD TO
KNOW.

Which scents are available?

Aloe, Argan and Bamboo. Each scent is presented as shampoo with the bamboo shell and as a 250 ml refill.

What comes with my first purchase?

For €12.50: the bamboo shell, reusable silicone cap and a filled 250 ml PET insert with a peel-off film seal.

How much is a refill — and what is included?

A 250 ml refill costs €4.50. It contains the filled, transparent PET insert with a film seal. Your bamboo shell and cap are reused.

How do I replace the insert?

Take off the cap, remove the empty insert and put the new refill in. Peel off the film seal completely before use, then put your existing cap back on.

Watch the refill change

Is the refill a flexible refill pouch?

It is a thin, transparent PET insert that fits directly inside the bamboo shell. Its upper flange rests on the tube rim and holds the film seal.

How does the dosing cap open?

The closure lifts out of the opening and turns aside. Its ring can rotate around the centre. The flexible centre itself belongs to the silicone cap and bends the surface when pressed.

Where can I find the ingredients?

Aloe, Argan and Bamboo are the scent names. Full ingredient lists and final directions will be added before sales begin.
