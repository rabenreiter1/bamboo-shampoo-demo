THE IDEA / BAMBOO SHAMPOO

Material with character.
Design with a plan.

A familiar moment in the bathroom. Rebuilt with bamboo, silicone and a replaceable PET insert.

ONE PRODUCT. MORE THAN ONE MOMENT.

Pick it up.
Pick it up again.

The bamboo shell is where the idea begins. Its grain and engraving stay visible. Inside, the filled insert can be changed; on top, the same cap goes back on.

The system has three main parts: shell, cap and refill. The way they come together makes the everyday ritual.

See the construction in motion

A CLOSER LOOK

Three parts.
One idea.

The construction behind the ritual.

01

The bamboo shell.

A cylindrical tube with laser engraving. Inside, it holds the PET insert. At the top, rim and cap meet.

02

The silicone cap.

The centre is part of the cap. Pressing it bends the silicone surface. The circular bearing lets the closure swivel around the centre.

PRESS TO DOSE.
03

The PET insert.

Thin and transparent. The upper flange rests on the bamboo shell. It holds the film that is peeled off completely before use.
